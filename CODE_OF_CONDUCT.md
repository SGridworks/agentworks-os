# Code of Conduct

AgentWorks OS follows a simple contributor standard:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Focus critique on the work, not the person.
- Assume public issue threads are permanent records.
- Do not post private data, credentials, customer data, or local incident details.
- Do not harass, threaten, or target contributors.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block users, or revoke contribution access when behavior makes collaboration unsafe or materially disrupts the project.
