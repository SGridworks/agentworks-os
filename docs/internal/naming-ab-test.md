# Naming + Positioning A/B Test Plan

## Objective

Test three candidate names/positioning lines with 2-3 prospects before locking customer-facing strings. Determine which framing drives the highest engagement (demo request or pilot commitment).

---

## Candidate Names + Taglines

| # | Name | Tagline | Rationale |
|---|------|---------|-----------|
| A | "AgentWorks OS" | "The AI platform for regulated businesses" | Platform-vision framing. Safe, broad, not yet contested. |
| B | "AI compliance firewall" | "Stop AI liability before it starts" | Sharp wedge. Compliance-first framing for regulated SMBs. |
| C | "Productivity layer for AI" | "Agentic control and automation" | Prospect-language framing. Specific, but not tied to a named customer. |

---

## What to Test

For each prospect, present one of the three framings in a single sentence, then measure:

1. **Comprehension** — Can the prospect immediately explain what the product does in their own words?
2. **Relevance** — Does the framing connect to a problem they currently have?
3. **Differentiation** — Does it feel distinct from tools they already know?
4. **Commitment** — Would they book a 30-minute demo to learn more?

---

## Test Script (5 minutes)

### Opening
> "Quick question — if I told you about a tool that [framing], what comes to mind?"

### Probe questions
- "What kind of business would use something like that?"
- "Does that connect to anything you're dealing with right now?"
- "What would need to be true for you to try it?"

### Close
> "If that matched something you'd want to explore, I'd love to show you a 20-minute demo. Would that be worth your time?"

---

## Prospect Criteria

- 5-50 person company
- Already using AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, etc.)
- In a regulated vertical: real estate, healthcare, finance, insurance, legal
- Decision-maker or heavy influencer (not a junior)
